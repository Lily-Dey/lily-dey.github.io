About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, working on scalable and interpretable trust-aware modeling for user-generated online content. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, NLP, and responsible AI.

With over a decade of industry experience spanning fintech, enterprise software, and AI research, I bring a rare combination of theoretical depth and practical engineering to complex problems. I've transitioned from leading software teams at Midland Bank to conducting cutting-edge ML research — a journey that has deeply shaped my perspective on building technology that genuinely serves people.

I'm passionate about AI transparency, ethical data practices, and making research accessible. As a Teaching Assistant for courses in security and database systems, I also work to instill responsible AI thinking in the next generation of CS students.
